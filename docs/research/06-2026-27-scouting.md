8. **Woltemade (NEW FWD £6.0m)** — pens + focal point at £6.0m, but buy for the GW3-6 run

## Promoted teams verdict

- **Coventry = this year's Sunderland** (Lampard stays, title by 11 pts, best defence, core
  intact + £22m Rushworth made permanent). Buy: Rushworth £4.5m GK, van Ewijk £4.0m (8
  assists from RB), Bobby Thomas £4.0m, Grimes £5.0m, Haji Wright £5.5m when fit. Sweet spot
  fixtures GW8-13; GW1 is Arsenal away.
- **Ipswich = hybrid**: £120m+ spent but manager discontinuity (McKenna → O'Neil) and the
  worst opening six in the league (Utd, Liverpool, Arsenal, City). Wait until ~GW7.
- **Hull = the Burnley analogue** as a team (1/4 relegation favourites, embargo, sold their
  keeper) but with real individual value: Egan £4.0m (10.1 CBIT/90 — cheapest DefCon in the
  game), Hughes £4.0m, McBurnie £5.5m (nailed, penalties). GK situation (Butland vs Tzolakis)
  unresolved — avoid until named.
